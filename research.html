“External Anchors, Policy Expectations, and the Lower Bound in Yield-Curve Forecasts” with Giuseppe Ragusa (Sapienza University of Rome)

Working Paper · September 2026

We anchor the 3-month yield of a Dynamic Nelson–Siegel model to external forecasts (federal funds futures and the Blue Chip consensus) and decompose the resulting forecast error to ask whether anchored yield-curve forecasts are limited by the information in the anchor or by the model that propagates it. Applying the decomposition to U.S. zero-coupon Treasury yields from 1991–2024, we find the answer depends on the monetary regime: before 2008 forecasts are limited by information, at the zero lower bound they are limited by the model, and after 2016 both anchors again improve on the random walk.
