About Me

I am a PhD student in Economics at Sapienza University of Rome, where I also completed my master's degree in Economics and Finance in a double-degree program with HEC Liège. Before that, I studied International Economics and Marketing at the University of Modena and Reggio Emilia.

My research interests lie at the intersection of applied macroeconomics, macroeconometrics, and financial macroeconomics, with a particular focus on the term structure of interest rates.
